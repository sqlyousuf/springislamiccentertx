    About us

    More than a place to pray

    The Klein Islamic Center serves the Muslim community of North Houston. It is a community center for everyone, committed to an Islamic way of life based on the Qur'an and the Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

    Daily prayer

    All five daily prayers are held in congregation, with iqamah times posted above.

    Qur'anic education

    Learning for students, young professionals and elders, with a focus on the Qur'an.

    Community

    A welcoming home for families and neighbors, and anyone who wants to learn about Islam.

    Support your masjid

    Donations keep the center running. On the donation page, choose KIC Musalla Expenses.
